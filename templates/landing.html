Core capabilities

Essential school operations, connected.

SMIS gives each authorized user a focused view of the records and workflows they are responsible for.

Student records

Maintain learner profiles, enrollment details, grade levels, sections, and status information.

Grade management

Support encoding, submission, registrar review, validation, and academic status monitoring.

Form 137 preparation

Prepare official academic records from validated student and grade information.

Reports and analytics

Review grade trends, section performance, subject outcomes, and students needing support.

Academic setup

Manage school years, grade levels, sections, subjects, periods, and teacher assignments.

Access and audit

Give administrators, teachers, registrars, and students the appropriate system access.

Designed around school work

From data entry to an accountable record.

Each step is organized around responsibility, helping school personnel move work forward without losing context.

  1. 01

    Record

    Encode student, academic, and grade information in the appropriate workspace.

  2. 02

    Review

    Check submissions, identify issues, and return records that require correction.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Confirm accurate academic information before it is used in official records.

  4. 04

    Report

    Use trusted information for Form 137 preparation and school performance reports.

About SMIS

A more orderly way to manage academic information.

The School Management Information System supports Paquito S. Yu Memorial National High School in maintaining consistent records and more transparent academic processes.

Its role-based workspaces help each user focus on the tasks and information relevant to their responsibilities.
